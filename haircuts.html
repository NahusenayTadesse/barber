An honest fit

Who this shop suits.

Every barbershop is right for somebody and wrong for somebody else. Here's ours, plainly.

  • People who'd rather have a barber than keep finding one. Most of the chairs here are filled by the same faces every three weeks, and that's the point.
  • Anyone who wants the truth about what will actually suit them. If the screenshot won't sit right on your hair, we'll say so and show you what will.
  • Textured and Afro hair, without the explaining. It's core work here, at the same price as anything else. No surcharge, no sighing, no being squeezed in.
  • People who want in, sharp, and out. Shop pace, not spa pace. A clean cut in forty minutes beats a perfect one in two hours.

Probably not the place for

Anyone after an hour of ceremony, a hot towel as theatre and a bill to match. There are excellent shops in London doing exactly that.

Anyone who wants the cheapest cut on the high street. We're fair, not the cheapest, and the difference shows.
